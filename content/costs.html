Use an official estimate process

In the US, if you are not using health insurance, providers usually must give a good faith estimate if you request one or schedule services at least 3 business days ahead. The estimate should itemize expected charges. It may not include separately scheduled services or other providers; request estimates from each relevant provider/facility. [[C1]]

Suggested pricing checklist

Project planning aid, not a statement of insurance coverage.

  • Exact service and provider; any facility or separate professional fee.
  • Initial evaluation versus follow-up price and cancellation terms.
  • Expected number of sessions only after clinical assessment.
  • In-network status, deductible, copay/coinsurance and authorization requirements.
  • Written estimate, its date and what it excludes.

Never delay emergency care to obtain a price estimate.
