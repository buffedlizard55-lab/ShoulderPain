MOUNT-SF1FB · Black

Folds away; does not quickly detach

Only a candidate if a permanent wall fixture is acceptable. Height is set by installation and the 18-inch depth needs a real screen/input-device fit check.

FULLY COLLAPSING · ELECTRIC$1,049

Lillipad PRO

Standard · Maple · displayed sale price

Desktop 46 × 24 inches; folded envelope 53 × 24 × 6 inches; unit weight 67 lb. The FAQ gives a suggested working-height range of 13.5–42 inches; 6 inches is the storage height. Wheels, a removable wheel brake and a 75 lb operational load rating are listed. [[P3]]

Storage fit and handling remain unproven

The collapsed desk is longer than its top. It is heavy; wheels do not guarantee easy movement through your room or across thresholds. Do not lift a heavy desk with a painful shoulder.

Availability flag: “Sold out,” add-to-cart and shipping messages appear together. Treat availability as unresolved. The same page lists 700 lb and 350 lb static-load tests in different sections; neither is the 75 lb operational rating. [[P3]]

Manual check: unplug and clear the top before moving; use two hands at the foot tube on flat indoor surfaces, and two people for stairs or uneven areas. The PRO manual limits operation to 30 lb from 13.5–20 inches and no load from 6–13.5 inches; 75 lb applies above 20 inches. It also requires wall clearance and prohibits an extension cord. [[P8]] Page advertises 60-day returns and a 5-year critical-component warranty. Check policy conditions. GO/Large pricing is not inferred from this selected variant. [[P3]]
