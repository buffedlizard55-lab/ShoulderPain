How verification was done

  • Medical claims: each claim was read from the cited page (direct retrieval where possible; otherwise from search-indexed full text of the page) on Sep 19, 2026, and the claim on this site is a paraphrase of that text. No claim was added without a cited source.
  • Product prices/specs: read from the official manufacturer product page's displayed price and specification blocks on Sep 19, 2026. Where a structured-data (schema.org) price was used, the displayed price on the same page was checked to agree.
  • Known limitations: (a) prices and stock change daily — the date above is the only guarantee; (b) some retailer/category pages rotate promotions; (c) affiliate review sites (Sleep Foundation) may bias picks — that's why manufacturer pages are the primary product source; (d) two URLs verified only via redirect chain (Cleveland Clinic mirror) were confirmed to resolve to the same canonical article.
  • Irregularities found during verification (full list on Next Steps): 404 on an old VIVO product URL (product moved); stale schema price-validity dates on some retailer pages; a review-site price ($40.81) that didn't match the retailer page ($39.99) for one roller — the retailer/official price was used instead.
