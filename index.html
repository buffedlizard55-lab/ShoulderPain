Get emergency help now

Sudden pressure or crushing shoulder pain, especially with chest pain, pain spreading to the jaw/arm/neck, breathlessness, dizziness or sweating, can signal a heart attack. Call 911 in the US or your local emergency number. Do not use this site to rule it out. [M1]
