The situation in one paragraph

I'm a healthy male, roughly 36–40, with pain in my left shoulder and a persistent “wing” feeling around the shoulder blade (scapula). Three likely contributors based on my lifestyle: (1) I sleep on my left side, (2) I work out and may have strained or injured the muscle group, and (3) I sit at a desk 12+ hours a day, sometimes slouched or frozen in one position. This project gathers the best-verified information on what could be causing this, what to do about it medically, a daily exercise plan, and a desk/furniture plan that fits a small room and can be folded away quickly.

Bottom line (verified takeaways)

  • Most shoulder pain is mechanical: rotator cuff irritation, bursitis/tendinitis, muscle strain, or postural overload — and posture + mechanics are explicitly listed as causes by MedlinePlus S1.
  • Long static sitting at a computer loads the neck and shoulder muscles that hold your head up; OSHA recommends breaking up static postures with micro-breaks and posture changes S16.
  • “Winging” of the shoulder blade can range from simple muscle weakness (improves with targeted exercise) to nerve involvement — a clinician should confirm which, especially if you see a visible protruding blade or weakness S28 S27.
  • Structured exercise and physical therapy are the mainstay of non-surgical treatment. An honest read of the evidence: a 2016 Cochrane review (findings re-verified Sep 2026) found pain outcomes “little or no different” from placebo (with a small function gain), a synthesis of Cochrane reviews rates specific exercise over non-specific exercise as high-quality evidence, and a 2023 randomized trial found a progressive scapular-stabilisation program did reduce symptoms in shoulder (subacromial) pain — exercise is the best long-term option, but expect slow, modest gains S22 S24 S23.
  • Sleep: the official self-care line is “lie either on the side that is not in pain or on your back” — and impingement-type shoulder pain is specifically aggravated by “lying on the involved side” S2 S45.
  • Desk: arms too high or too low, armrests set too high, and a mouse placed too far away are each named shoulder-pain causes in state OSHA guidance — the cheapest fixes on the whole site S21.
  • There are genuine foldable, small-space desk options from ~$54 to ~$887, verified on manufacturer pages S33 S34 S32.
