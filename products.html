FLIP-TOP · COMPACT$54.49

COMHOMA DH005

White · no-drawer version · sale price

Manufacturer images list a 31.5 × 15.75 inch top, 30.71–46.46 inch height range, lockable casters and a 90° flip-top storage design. Stated capacity: 110 lb. [P1]

Conditional candidate

The shallow surface and high minimum height may not fit your seated setup. The base remains on the floor; the full folded envelope and unit weight were not verified. “Folds flat” marketing does not mean the entire frame collapses.

Flag: drawer-variant images mix different heights/capacities into the same page. Do not transfer these White/no-drawer specs to the drawer version. Monitor-arm compatibility is unverified. [P1]

The $25.50 figure near the images is the discount, not the price; the selected White price is $54.49. [P1]

WALL-FOLDING · FIXED INSTALL$69.99

VIVO folding workbench

MOUNT-SF1FB · Black

43.3 × 18 inch top, 1 inch thick; 2.3 inches from the wall when folded. Steel brackets and particle-board top; stated capacity 110 lb. Mounting options: wood stud, brick or concrete. Page showed in stock, a 3-year warranty and 30-day return policy. [P2]

Folds away; does not quickly detach

Only a candidate if a permanent wall fixture is acceptable. Height is set by installation and the 18-inch depth needs a real screen/input-device fit check.

Flag: confirm wall structure, installation permission and the manual’s requirements. Installation cost is not verified. No generic clamp-on monitor arm is recommended for this folding particle-board top.

FULLY COLLAPSING · ELECTRIC$1,049

Lillipad PRO

Standard · Maple · displayed sale price

Desktop 46 × 24 inches; folded envelope 53 × 24 × 6 inches; unit weight 67 lb. The FAQ gives a suggested working-height range of 13.5–42 inches; 6 inches is the storage height. Wheels, a removable wheel brake and a 75 lb operational load rating are listed. [P3]

Storage fit and handling remain unproven

The collapsed desk is longer than its top. It is heavy; wheels do not guarantee easy movement through your room or across thresholds. Do not lift a heavy desk with a painful shoulder.

Availability flag: “Sold out,” add-to-cart and shipping messages appear together. Treat availability as unresolved. The same page lists 700 lb and 350 lb static-load tests in different sections; neither is the 75 lb operational rating. [P3]

Page advertises 60-day returns and a 5-year critical-component warranty. Check the linked policy conditions. GO/Large pricing is not inferred from this selected variant. [P3]

IKEA GUNDE

Black · article 002.177.97

Open size 16⅛ W × 17¾ D × 30¾ H inches; fixed seat height 17¾ inches. Tested for 220 lb. Pre-assembled, indoor use, locks open and has a carry/hanging handle. Local stock requires a store/delivery check. [P4]
