Oregon OSHA itself suggests “mounting the monitor on an adjustable arm” when workstation space is short S21. Your monitor must have VESA 75/100 holes on the back (most do; check the spec sheet).

Cheaper first step (no purchase): MedlinePlus — “When you sleep, lie either on the side that is not in pain or on your back. Resting your painful shoulder on a couple of pillows may help” S2; add a pillow between your knees and a pillow that keeps the head and neck in line with the body — the positioning advice from Cleveland Clinic and the Sleep Foundation S13 S31. The arm-hole design targets the specific failure mode of side sleeping: the top arm trapped under the pillow, pulling the shoulder forward and stretching the joint all night (manufacturer claims — treat as unverified until you try it).
