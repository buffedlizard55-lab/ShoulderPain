Requirement check: small space + foldable / quick-removal The desk options below all fold, fold-flat, or lift away quickly (the hard requirement). Chairs are the exception: a chair suitable for 12 hours of computer work does not fold flat — the mitigation is choosing a compact 5-star-base chair that tucks under the desk. This trade-off is flagged for review on the Next Steps page.

Cheaper first step (no purchase): MedlinePlus — “When you sleep, lie either on the side that is not in pain or on your back. Resting your painful shoulder on a couple of pillows may help” S2; add a pillow between your knees and a pillow that keeps the head and neck in line with the body — the positioning advice from Cleveland Clinic and the Sleep Foundation S13 S31. The arm-hole design is a manufacturer-positioning feature for side sleepers; it is not evidence that the pillow treats shoulder pain. Use the return/trial policy and stop if the arrangement increases pain, numbness, or tingling.
