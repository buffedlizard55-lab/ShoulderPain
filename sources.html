Sources & audit

Follow every source.

Claim-level references, short evidence excerpts and explicit limits.

What “checked” means here

The cited official page was opened and the relevant passage compared with the guide’s wording. It does not mean independent clinical validation, a guaranteed price, a systematic review or a guarantee of zero errors. This project has not been reviewed by your clinician.

How to review a statement

  1. Use the reference beside the statement to open the official page directly.
  2. Use the register below for the section, short evidence excerpt and scope limit.
  3. Treat “project suggestion,” “project application” and “unresolved” as editorial judgments, not findings asserted by the publisher.
